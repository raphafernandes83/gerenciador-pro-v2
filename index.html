For those still learning

You will get a lot wrong at the start. That was never the problem.

People learning chart analysis are right about half the time. That is normal, and it is nothing to be ashamed of — it is the price of learning to read a chart. What breaks a beginner's bankroll is not the hit rate. It is what they do after the fourth loss in a row.

At an 87% payout, every $1 lost costs $1.15 to win back. Four losses demand five wins, and five wins in a row show up only 10.9% of the time. That distance is what makes the hand rise on its own and double the entry.

Doubling after a loss is the most repeated and most expensive advice in this market. We simulated 200,000 sessions under the conditions described below: classic martingale broke the bankroll in 67% of them. Two out of every three.

Gerenciador PRO splits the recovery instead of doubling it. Across those same 200,000 sessions, the bankroll broke in 12% — and the median result ended at $101.76, against $87.00 for flat-entry trading.

That is at a 50% hit rate, in the dark. Reach 60% and the break rate falls to 1.4%.

No money management invents profit, and anyone promising you that is lying. Below a 53.48% hit rate, time works against you — over 500 operations at 50%, the break rate climbs to 47%. Gerenciador PRO does not change that math. It buys you room to learn before the bankroll runs out.

67% Bankroll broken with classic martingale
12% Bankroll broken with Gerenciador PRO
The conditions these numbers were calculated under

Starting bankroll of $100. Base entry of $2, equal to 2% of the bankroll. Payout of 87%. Hit rate of 50%, the equivalent of a coin. 100 operations per session. 200,000 sessions simulated for each system.

The figures are proportional: a $1,000 bankroll with a $20 entry produces exactly the same result. What matters is the ratio between entry and bankroll, not the currency or the size.

The payout changes the outcome. At 80%, the Gerenciador PRO break rate rises to 17.4%; at 95%, it drops to 7.0%. Classic martingale stays above 64% at any payout between 70% and 95%.

Guided Mode

The system reads each result, blocks out-of-sequence commands and keeps only the next step in the plan available.

  • Reduces mistakes during the session

Recovery Cycles

Fixed Hand, Reinvestment, Recovery and a progression of up to 30 hands, all calculated automatically.

  • Back to the cycle only after recovering

Adaptive Compounding

The next entries follow the real state of the bankroll: they rise as capital grows and shrink while there is a loss to recover.
